Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MediQueue

How do I book an appointment? ▼
Register or login to your account, browse available doctors, select your preferred doctor, choose a date and time slot, and confirm your booking. You will receive instant confirmation.
Can I cancel my appointment? ▼
Yes. Go to your patient dashboard, find the appointment you want to cancel, and click the Cancel button. Cancellations are free of charge.
Is my personal data safe? ▼
Absolutely. We use industry standard encryption, bcrypt password hashing, JWT secure tokens, and role-based access control to keep your data completely safe.
How do I register as a doctor? ▼
Go to the Register page, select the Doctor role, fill in your details including specialization, and submit. Your account will be created immediately.
Is MediQueue free to use? ▼
Yes. Registering and booking appointments on MediQueue is completely free for patients. No hidden charges.
What specializations are available? ▼
We cover Cardiology, Neurology, Dermatology, Orthopedics, Ophthalmology, Dentistry, Pulmonology, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Gynecology and more.
